A FEW QUESTIONS

Before you hop in.

Do I have to download anything?

No separate desktop app is required. Open LiveChat in your browser using the link on this page.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes, the website is designed for phone and desktop browsers. Some browser features, like notifications and calls, depend on your device's permissions.

What's the difference between this page and LiveChat?

This is the public homepage. The actual messaging app is at grinchtaffy.github.io/livechat/, where you can sign in and start chatting.

Can anyone read my chats from this homepage?

No messages are displayed here. This public page only explains the app; conversations are accessed in LiveChat after signing in.

Where are the extra tools?

Once you're inside the app, open Settings and look under Apps & Connections. Available tools may differ by account.
